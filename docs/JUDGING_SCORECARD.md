**Problem statement:** SIH 26189 — AI-Powered Criminal Network Analysis System  
**Assessment date:** 05 September 2026  
**Claim boundary:** deployable investigative pilot evaluated on supplied and explicitly synthetic data; not an accredited police system.

## Five claims to demonstrate

1. **Multi-source evidence is real application logic:** 32 records from six channels are classified and fused at runtime.
2. **Patterns are computed, not narrated:** five declared patterns are recovered by deterministic detectors; each exposes records, alternatives and a next verification action.
3. **Every path is challengeable:** all 148 Operation Suraksha relationships carry source ID, channel, time and SHA-256 record digest.
4. **Safety changes system behavior:** protected people are a separate type, stay masked, receive risk zero, and never enter ML; uncertain identities are not auto-merged.
5. **The team knows what its model cannot prove:** the leakage audit quarantines the perfect reproduction result, the claim passport marks field accuracy unestablished, and operational use remains blocked pending independent validation.
6. **Blockchain is used only where it adds trust:** a nonce-blinded audit checkpoint is externally witnessed; calendar-pending and Bitcoin-confirmed states are never conflated.

## Do not claim

- That the fictional scenario is NCRB operational data.
- That GraphSAGE predicts criminality or achieves 100% real-world accuracy.
- That graph connectivity proves association, intent or guilt.
- That evidence is stored on-chain, that calendar acceptance is Bitcoin confirmation, or that the pilot is an agency-approved immutable ledger.
- That a competition build is production accredited.
